## Week 2 Day 2 — What we’re learning (goal first)
Goal: make DI lifetimes real , not just words.

We’re going to answer this with proof:

- When I register a service as Transient / Scoped / Singleton , how many instances get created and when ?
### Expected observations
- Scoped (per request):
  - Inside 1 request, resolving ITaskService twice gives the same instance .
  - Across two separate HTTP calls, you get different instances .
- Transient (every resolve):
  - Inside 1 request, resolving twice gives two different instances .
- Singleton (one for app lifetime):
  - Inside 1 request: same instance
  - Across many requests: same instance
